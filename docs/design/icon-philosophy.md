# Vermilion Kin

Vermilion Kin treats identity as a small living seal: one compact form, immediately
legible, yet warm enough to feel inhabited. Space is disciplined around a single
friendly gesture. Curves are broad, optical balance is painstaking, and every
silhouette must remain unmistakable when reduced to the size of a menu-bar mark.

Color carries the emotional hierarchy. Lacquer red provides confidence and memory;
warm cream softens it into companionship; ink brown adds just enough structure to
avoid sweetness without weight. Highlights should feel like enamel catching real
light, calibrated with master-level restraint rather than decorative gloss.

The central figure is unmistakably Xiaoxiao: a smiling face, short ink-black hair,
warm cream coat, and paired white flower tassels. The crop stays close so expression
arrives before detail, while the character accessories preserve her identity at
small sizes. No wordmark competes with the face; the person is the mark.

Scale creates the system. The same mark must survive as a 16-pixel favicon, a macOS
application icon, a navigation signature, and a large launch asset without changing
its character. Edges, negative space, and internal contrast require meticulous
craftsmanship so the identity remains crisp rather than collapsing into detail.

Composition stays calm and frontal. One asymmetric accent adds life while the larger
mass remains centered and stable. No text is needed inside the object; form and color
do the speaking. The final artifact should feel painstakingly made by someone at the
top of their field: tactile, native, personal, and quietly impossible to confuse.
